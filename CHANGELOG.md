# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First public release for WoW Forever (interface 16001).
- Movable round clock icon; left click opens a menu with three toggles.
- Right click opens settings: icon size, opacity, lock, tooltip, reset position.
- Auto loot, auto accept quests, auto turn in quests.
- Hold Shift to skip automation once.
- English and Russian localization.
